01 · Problem

A fake QR leads to a fake site.

A QR code is only an address. Whoever scans it trusts the page that opens, and anyone can build a page. Without a signature a green tick proves nothing.

Today: trusting an address

1The QR on the certificate is scanned 2https://rnarkaz.example/check 3“Certificate is valid”

The page copies the real one: from a distance rn looks like m. The checker is fooled.

With Sanad: checking the signature

1The QR holds the signed certificate itself 2did:web:markaz.example · key cached 3Forged copy: signature does not match

The checker visits no website. The signature is checked mathematically: change one letter and the result is “forged”.

Source: Youth Affairs Agency, gov.uz (2024-07-08): forgers “created a fake site similar to the real one and linked it to their fake QR code”. gov.uz/en/yoshlar/news/view/15837. The addresses in the example are made up (.example).

02 · How it works

Issue, hold, verify.

Sanad is Arabic for “support”; in hadith studies it is the chain of who confirmed it from whom. Here the chain has three links.

Issue

Training centre

Upload the list of graduates (CSV). Each certificate is signed with the centre’s key as a W3C Verifiable Credential.

did:web:markaz.example

Hold

Graduate

The graduate keeps it: a QR in a PDF, in Telegram or as a file. No website or account is needed.

QR = signed data

Verify

Employer, HR

Show the QR to the Telegram bot or the web verifier. The signature is checked on the spot against a cached key, in seconds.

genuine · forged · revoked
Even without internet

Checking the signature needs no network. Internet is only for one optional step: has the issuer revoked the certificate since (Bitstring Status List)?

    05 · Who it is for

    Those who issue, hold and check.

    Sanad is for private organisations’ own certificates. It does not issue or imitate state documents (diplomas, IDs).

    Issuer

    Training centres and IT courses

    Forging your certificates becomes pointless. Hundreds of signed certificates from one CSV, and a wrong one can be revoked.

    CSV → signed VC → PDF + QR

    Issuer

    Universities and events

    Continuing-education courses, trainings, olympiad and hackathon certificates.

    Not state diplomas: official systems handle those.

    Open Badges 3.0

    Verifier

    Employers and HR

    Check a candidate’s certificate in a minute, without trusting any website.

    06 · Open standards

    Nothing home-made.

    Sanad builds only on open, published standards. A certificate stays verifiable even if Sanad shuts down: any compatible software can read it.

    08 · Honest status

    What exists today, and what does not.

    Wonmor will never ask you for money, passwords or keys. Official addresses: sanad.wonmor.io and github.com/wonmortech.

    Exists

    • Research: competitors, standards, legal state
    • Plan and architecture
    • This page and the in-browser demo

    Not yet

    • Issuer service and CLI
    • Telegram bot
    • Any issued certificate or user

    Limits

    • A Sanad signature is not, and does not replace, an EIMZO electronic digital signature (law ZRU-793)
    • The signature proves who issued it, not what the person knows
    • Legal questions are with a lawyer; awaiting answers
